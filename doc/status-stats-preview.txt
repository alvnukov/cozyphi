==================================
Reproduce (fixture data only):
  go test -mod=readonly ./internal/tui/statuspane -run TestStats -v

Tests cover RGB levels (#874620, #c97030, #ffad55), fixed weekday rows,
preserved empty columns, responsive metrics and fixed footer scrolling at
80x24 and 40x24. TestStatsFixedMonthColumns uses an independent, tabulated
month-column oracle at 2026-01-04 UTC, not the production month algorithm.
TestStatsMidnightWakeAndStreak advances a controlled clock across UTC midnight.

Empty complete-snapshot cells are muted; future dates are blank. Partial
snapshots instead show ? for cells without observed rounds, with these lines
BEFORE the calendar (wrapped to the viewport):
  Partial history — some observations are unavailable
  ? no observed rounds; coverage incomplete

Period-excluded dates use · and a pre-calendar legend:
  · outside selected period (cutoff YYYY-MM-DD)
The cutoff stays at the request date until refresh: no independent all-time
calendar data is synthesized from period-filtered history.

Loading/unavailable snapshots show the period selector and explanation only:
  [Overview]  Models
  [All time]  Last 7 days  Last 30 days
  Loading history…
No calendar, zero-valued metrics or intensity legend is drawn in that state.

At 40 columns the footer remains on the final viewport row while scrolling:
  p period · m view · r refresh · Esc
At 80 columns:
  p period · m Overview/Models · r refresh · Esc close

Config now explicitly labels automatic compaction as:
  Compaction reminder: effective unavailable; configured automatic default
and agent pins as:
  Configured agent model pins · effective unavailable
General source paths remain visible; winning source is explicitly unavailable.
